Compliance and document review

Compliance and Document Review for Custom Stickers

Some buyers need quality, material, chemical, kids-market, order inspection or shipment files before confirming custom sticker production. File availability must match the actual material, ink, adhesive, finish, application and destination market.

Ask About DocumentsReview Materials
FSC Paper Source CheckFor paper stickers, kraft labels, stationery sheets and paper packaging labels when paper-source files are requested.
SGS / TUV Report ReviewFor third-party test reports connected to specific materials, finishes or buyer compliance files.
RoHS / REACH RequestFor chemical compliance requests that depend on material, usage and destination-market requirements.
ISO 9001 File CheckFor supplier review folders and quality-system document checks requested by B2B buyers.
EN71 / CPSIA ReviewFor kids stickers, reward stickers and classroom projects where target-market safety documents may be needed.
COC / QC Document PackFor production inspection, packing review, shipment document and project-specific QC requests.

Important limitation

Documents Must Match the Actual Order

One file does not automatically cover every sticker product. Buyers should share the material, adhesive, ink, finish, use case and destination market before any compliance statement is used in sales or packaging communication.

What to send

Information Needed for Review

  • Sticker material and finish preference
  • Application surface and target market
  • Kids-market, chemical or paper-source requirements
  • Quantity, packing method and shipping destination
  • Any buyer checklist or supplier audit request
