Promotional Sticker Reprints: Plan the Quantity Needed First

Published November 17, 2026.

By ZC Labels · B2B procurement guide · Sources and editorial approach

In this guide
  1. Calculate the usable gap for each deadline
  2. Compare the routes that can meet the actual use date
  3. Give the corrected delivery its own release

When a sticker delivery is short or part of it is held for inspection, work out how many accepted units each design needs at the next deadline. Use that gap to decide whether to request a targeted reprint, redistribute approved stock, or change the rollout. Keep the balance of the original order visible until the parties agree what should happen to it.

Calculate the usable gap for each deadline

Start with the released order and list what the team needs when the campaign or packing run starts. Count in the finished unit the team can use. If a handout includes a fixed assortment, count complete accepted sets. Extra pieces of one design cannot fill a shortage of another. Mark disputed or unchecked stock separately until the responsible buyer decides its disposition. Use the bulk receiving guide to record the underlying counts.

For example, a launch needs 300 finished packs and the buyer has accepted 240. The immediate gap is 60 packs. If the original order calls for 500 packs, the outstanding order quantity and the 60-pack launch gap answer different questions. Record who can set priorities for the remaining 200 after the launch quantity is covered. Confirm which existing stock can be moved without creating a shortage at another location.

Ask when corrected packs can be ready for use at the receiving point. Include proof review, packing, transport and time to check the delivery. Confirm the agreed scope, costs and treatment of the remainder for the actual order. ZC's terms call for written confirmation of final specifications, schedule and shipping. ZC terms of service.

Give the corrected delivery its own release

Keep the original lot identity, retained evidence and the new proof linked to the recovery plan. Give the replacement cartons a reference the receiving team can identify. Check the corrected feature before releasing the new packs, and agree how held stock will be handled. Bosch's supplier quality guide illustrates how one buyer specifies sorting and identification during defect resolution. Its requirements apply to its own suppliers. Bosch supplier quality guide, section 11.

Send the affected order and artwork references, accepted quantity and immediate gap by design, required ready-for-use date and destination. If you have a corrected proof or an identified observation, include it so the team can discuss available options for your promotional sticker project.
