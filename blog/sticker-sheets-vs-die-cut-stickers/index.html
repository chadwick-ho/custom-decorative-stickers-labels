Sticker Sheets vs Die-Cut Stickers: Which Format Fits Your Project?

A practical comparison for stationery, kids, packaging, gift, holiday, and promotional sticker projects.

Sticker sheets and die-cut stickers can both work well for B2B custom sticker projects, but they serve different buying goals. The best format depends on artwork count, application, packaging style, and how the stickers will be used.

Sticker Sheets

Sticker sheets are useful when one product needs multiple small designs on a single backing sheet. They are common for planner stickers, kids reward stickers, activity packs, craft products, seasonal collections, and stationery sets.

Die-Cut Stickers

Die-cut stickers are individually shaped around the artwork outline. They are often used for logo stickers, promotional giveaways, packaging inserts, event stickers, and decorative brand stickers.

What to Confirm

  • How many individual designs are included
  • Whether the buyer wants one sheet or separate stickers
  • Final application surface and handling
  • Packaging requirement and target quantity
  • Cut line spacing and proof review details
