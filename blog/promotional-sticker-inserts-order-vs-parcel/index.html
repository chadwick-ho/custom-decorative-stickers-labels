Promotional Sticker Inserts: Count Orders or Parcels?

Published November 12, 2026.

By ZC Labels · B2B procurement guide · Sources and editorial approach

In this guide
  1. Choose the event that qualifies the insert
  2. Rehearse exceptions before choosing the bundle size
  3. Turn the approved rule into a purchase quantity

Choose the unit that earns a giveaway sticker before quoting the print run. If the campaign offers one insert per eligible order, nominate one parcel to carry it and give the packing team a way to see that it has already been included. If the campaign calls for one insert in every eligible parcel, count parcels, including any planned split shipments.

Choose the event that qualifies the insert

Write one sentence the campaign and fulfillment owners can approve. For example, a campaign might offer one sticker per paid order placed during its published window. A different campaign might add a sticker to each qualifying parcel shipped during a warehouse promotion. Record the relevant channel, cutoff time and time zone alongside that sentence. Decide how to handle an order placed before the cutoff but packed after it. In the first example the order date matters. In the second the dispatch date matters.

Check whether the insert is intended for the purchaser or for everyone opening a parcel. One per order can suit a single promised bonus, with a designated parcel to deliver it. A parcel rule can suit a campaign intended to reach each recipient in a multi-address order. That choice increases consumption when more packages are sent. If all qualifying orders always ship in one parcel, the quantities match until the first split or replacement.

Rehearse exceptions before choosing the bundle size

Packing situationDecision to recordHold the insert if
Two parcels for one orderUnder an order rule, designate the parcel that should carry the insert and record completion against that order.Two stations could both claim the same order or neither can identify the designated parcel.
Backorder crosses the cutoffUse the qualifying event chosen by the campaign owner. Confirm whether the original order remains eligible and which parcel will carry the piece.The published wording and the packing instruction use different dates.
Replacement or cancelled parcelDefine whether a replacement receives another insert and what record controls that choice. Reassign an unfulfilled insert only with an approved rule.The earlier parcel's insert status is unknown and the campaign requires one per order.
Several packing stationsAssign the correct revision and quantities by station. Rehearse one ordinary shipment and one split order using the actual pick instructions.Packers cannot distinguish the eligible task or confirm the piece before closure.

A paper checklist can serve a single station if it travels with the order and records where the piece went. For parallel stations, agree how the team will share that record before ordering station bundles. The method belongs to the buyer's operation. A sticker proof can confirm the artwork and delivered format, while the packing rehearsal checks how the chosen rule works.

Turn the approved rule into a purchase quantity

In an illustrative plan, 240 eligible orders include 30 orders that each split into two eligible parcels. One piece per order calls for 240 pieces. One piece per eligible parcel calls for 270. Use quantities for the approved case, then show any agreed spare allowance separately. Allocate that total by artwork and by station if separate bundles help the team. Confirm whether the supplier quotes individual backed pieces, complete sheets or finished packs. ZC promotional sticker formats.

Provide the expected number of qualifying orders or parcels, the split and replacement rule, the artwork, finished dimensions and the requested bundle counts by station. Ask which counts and packing scope the quotation covers. ZC shipping information.
