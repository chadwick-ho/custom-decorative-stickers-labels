Project decisions

Choose the Supply Route for Your Bulk Order

Start with what the receiving team needs: individual pieces for distribution, multiple designs on a sheet, or printed labels for repeated application. Keep quantity per design separate from the combined total so the quote describes the stock you actually need.

For a wholesale or repeat project, include artwork versions, final dimensions, surface, delivery format, packing and destination. Pricing, material suitability, order quantity and timing are confirmed for the actual project; a sample image does not establish those terms.

Roll labels or sticker sheets?

What does a proof approve?

A digital proof records artwork and identified instructions. Physical appearance and use questions need references that represent those conditions.

Which material and adhesive?

Identify the actual contact surface, coating, application conditions and intended use before comparing proposed constructions.
