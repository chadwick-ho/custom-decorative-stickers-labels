How to Add a Cutline to Sticker Artwork

A buyer-friendly checklist for clearer custom sticker cutline review.

By ZC Labels Production Team · Reviewed for B2B sticker sourcing.

A cutline translates a visual design into a physical edge. It tells the production process where a sticker should separate from surrounding material. The line itself is not decoration, so a good proof makes the relationship between artwork, border and finished size easy to understand.

Die-Cut, Kiss-Cut and Sheet Cutlines

FormatWhat the cutline controlsWhat the buyer should review
Die-cut stickerThe outer shape of the finished piece and backingContour, border, narrow details and handling
Kiss-cut stickerThe peelable sticker while extra backing remainsPeel area, backing shape and cut depth direction
Sticker sheetEach sticker shape within one backing sheetSpacing, design count, margins and sheet layout
Roll labelLabel edge within repeated roll spacingFinal dimensions, gap, orientation and roll direction

Build the Cutline Around the Finished Use

Begin with the intended size. A contour that looks elegant on a large screen may produce fragile points or crowded gaps at a small printed size. Simplifying a tiny indentation can improve handling without changing the recognizable silhouette.

Keep important text, faces, logos and fine details away from the intended edge. The proof should show whether the border is white, transparent, colored or intentionally absent. If the sticker goes on a colored surface, the edge direction can change the final visual result.

Complex Shapes Need Practical Judgment

Hair, leaves, stars, handwritten lettering and character accessories can create many small protrusions. Not every visual detail needs its own physical contour. A smoother cutline may be easier to peel, apply and pack while still preserving the artwork identity.

Sticker Sheet Spacing Is Part of the Layout

For sheets, the cutline cannot be reviewed in isolation. Confirm the number of stickers, backing dimensions, spacing for peeling, edge margins and any printed header or brand area. If customers use the sheet repeatedly, leave a layout that remains understandable after some stickers are removed.

Proof Checklist

  • Correct artwork and final dimensions
  • Cutline follows the intended shape without trimming key content
  • Border direction is visible and consistent
  • Small points and gaps are practical at final size
  • Sheet spacing or roll orientation matches the finished format
  • Any factory-proposed adjustment is identified before approval
