Custom Die-Cut Stickers

Die-cut stickers are individually cut around the artwork outline. They work well for logo stickers, character stickers, event giveaways, packaging inserts and retail sticker packs when buyers want a finished sticker shape instead of a rectangular label.

FAQ

Custom Die-Cut Stickers Questions

Can you review the cutline for die-cut stickers?

Yes. Send artwork and size details so the cutline, border width and shape can be checked before quoting.

Can die-cut stickers be packed individually?

Packing can be discussed by project, including loose bulk supply, OPP bags or retail sticker pack requirements.

Which material is best for die-cut stickers?

Material should be confirmed by application. Vinyl, paper, clear film and holographic options can be reviewed based on use and budget.
