How Much Do Custom Stickers Cost?

A practical pricing checklist for B2B custom sticker inquiries.

Custom sticker cost depends on confirmed specifications, not a single universal price. Buyers usually need to clarify artwork, size, quantity, material, finish, format, packing and destination before a realistic quote can be prepared.

What Changes the Quote?

  • Sticker size, shape and total quantity
  • Die-cut, kiss-cut, roll label or sticker sheet format
  • Paper, vinyl, clear film, holographic or other material options
  • Gloss, matte, foil, lamination or special finish needs
  • Retail packing, OPP bags, sheet sets or roll direction requirements
  • Shipping country and deadline if time-sensitive

How to Get a Better Quote

Send artwork or a reference image, estimated size, quantity, application and material preference. If you are not sure, explain where the sticker will be used and which visual effect you want.
