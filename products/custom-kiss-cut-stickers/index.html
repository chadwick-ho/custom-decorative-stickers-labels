Custom Kiss-Cut Stickers

Kiss-cut stickers separate the peelable sticker shape from the retained backing. An individual sticker can sit on a larger backing, or several stickers can share a sheet. Start here to specify the cut and backing relationship; use the sticker-sheet page when the main task is ordering a multi-design sheet or retail set.

Buyer fit

Who This Format Fits

Brands and product teams that need a defined peelable contour, retained backing, grip area or delicate artwork handling. Single backed pieces and multi-design sheets require different layout and packing instructions.

Quote inputs

Information to Clarify

  • Peelable sticker contour and final dimensions
  • Outer backing size and shape, identified separately
  • Grip area, border and small-detail cut review
  • Single backed pieces or multiple stickers on one sheet
  • Quantity per design and packing format

FAQ

Custom Kiss-Cut Stickers Questions

What is the difference between die-cut and kiss-cut stickers?

Die-cut stickers are cut around the full sticker shape. Kiss-cut stickers remain on a backing sheet, making them useful for multi-design sheets.

Can one sheet include many designs?

Yes. Send the sheet size, artwork count and spacing requirements for proof review.

Can small text be used on kiss-cut sheets?

Small text should be checked at final size during proof review to confirm readability.

Project decisions

Approve Two Shapes: the Sticker and Its Backing

Mark the peelable artwork contour and outer backing as separate boundaries. The backing can carry an intentional grip area or additional design, but its dimensions must not be mistaken for the finished sticker size. Review small projections and peel access at physical size.

Kiss-cut describes the cut relationship; it does not tell the supplier how many designs form a sheet or set. For multiple stickers on one backing, continue to the sheet page and supply the sheet size, component count and sheets per pack. For an individual giveaway, specify the backed piece and its packed quantity.
