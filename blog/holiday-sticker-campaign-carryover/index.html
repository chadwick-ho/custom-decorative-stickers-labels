Holiday Stickers: Plan What Can Carry Over

Published November 11, 2026.

By ZC Labels · B2B procurement guide · Sources and editorial approach

In this guide
  1. Give leftover stock a named next use
  2. Price the finished gift and the changeover
  3. Release the stock before the next event

Decide which parts of a holiday sticker order can still be used after the campaign closes. Put the date, promotion or local event wording on a separate sticker or printed card when the buyer has a confirmed second use for the undated artwork. Choose a single campaign design when the date is part of the finished look or the packing team needs to apply everything in one step.

Give leftover stock a named next use

Start with the next gift, display or event that would actually use the artwork. A snowflake without a year may work for a winter gift box later in the same season. A design naming one venue may have no second use even if it contains no date. Review the words, illustration, language and brand together. Ask the campaign owner to confirm that they will still be appropriate for the later use and that the artwork authorization covers it.

Write down the next surface and opening method too. A round seal chosen for a coated gift box might land across a fold on a later paper bag. The later package needs its own fit and application review if it changes. When no second use has an owner, count the stock for the current campaign only. The custom holiday stickers page lists seasonal seals, sheets and retail packs and asks buyers to specify quantities by artwork and how the pieces will be supplied.

Price the finished gift and the changeover

Ask for two comparable configurations using the same number of gifts, destination and required receipt date. In the combined design, count one dated seal per gift if that is the planned application. In the split design, count the undated seals and the event-specific pieces separately. Include the person responsible for applying the second piece and where it goes. If the campaign wording can be carried by an existing insert, compare that route before asking for a second sticker.

For an illustrative run of 300 gifts, the split route may need 300 motif seals and 300 event pieces. If the event ends early, count each remainder by revision. A leftover event sticker cannot be allocated to the next event just because the plain seal can. Compare quoted charges and the buyer's application effort before deciding how many undated seals to buy. An identified future use does not settle the supplier's minimum, packing or freight terms. Confirm those in the project quotation. ZC shipping information.

Release the stock before the next event

Keep the accepted artwork reference and received batch identity with the undated stock. At closeout, record the count that actually remains and keep dated pieces out of the carryover group. Before reuse, confirm the next artwork and application are still in scope and check representative pieces against the intended package. Inspect their condition and follow any applicable storage or age guidance for the supplied construction. If the lot is unidentified, storage is unclear or the test piece behaves differently from the accepted reference, hold that stock for review. The retained count is a planning input until those checks pass.

For a quote, provide the current artwork split, the proposed later use, quantity by component, and a photo or drawing of each placement. State whether the event wording must appear on the seal, a second sticker or an insert.
