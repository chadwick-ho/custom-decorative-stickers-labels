How to Prepare Artwork for Custom Sticker Production

A buyer-focused checklist for sending clearer custom sticker requirements before quote review.

Good artwork preparation helps a custom sticker project move from inquiry to proof review with fewer delays. Before requesting a quote, prepare your final artwork if available, or send draft artwork and reference images if the design is still being developed.

Information to Include

  • Sticker type, such as die-cut stickers, sticker sheets, roll labels, or decorative labels
  • Approximate size and preferred shape
  • Estimated order quantity
  • Intended application, such as packaging, stationery, event giveaways, or gift decoration
  • Material and finish preferences if known
  • Any packing or sheet layout requirement

Artwork Rights

Submitted artwork should be original, customer-owned, or properly authorized for reproduction. Avoid using protected characters, brand logos, or third-party artwork unless you have the legal right to reproduce them.
