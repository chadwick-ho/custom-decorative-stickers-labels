Event Sticker Orders: Confirm the Venue Receiving Handoff

Published November 15, 2026.

By ZC Labels · B2B procurement guide · Sources and editorial approach

In this guide
  1. Choose the receiving route from the show instructions
  2. Give the carton a recipient who can act
  3. Check readiness before the opening day

Before asking a printer to deliver giveaway stickers to an exhibition, get the event's current receiving instructions and name the person who will collect the stock for the booth. Confirm the permitted receiving location and window before the dispatch instruction is released. A delivery at the venue still needs a defined path to the team handing out the stickers.

Choose the receiving route from the show instructions

An event may offer an advance warehouse route, direct delivery during move-in, or another arrangement for small parcels. Read the actual instructions before selecting one. Freeman's exhibitor guide describes advance-warehouse receiving windows, direct-to-show deliveries during move-in and material handling as distinct parts of the event route. The organizer or appointed handler confirms which arrangements apply to the show. Freeman exhibitor guide.

For a small giveaway order, compare the event's accepted parcel route with delivery to a business location followed by a team member carrying the stock to the booth. The latter can give the team an opportunity to check the contents before travel, but someone must own that final transport and have the space to carry it. Direct event delivery may reduce that step while adding receiving requirements, a collection task and possible handling charges. Obtain the proposed costs and responsibilities for the actual route.

Give the carton a recipient who can act

Ask the exhibitor or logistics owner for the approved recipient format, event and company references, booth identification where required, and an on-site contact. Use the handler's actual label or document requirements. Share real delivery details through the order channel. Keep a carton count and contents reference with the booth team so they can recognize the shipment and check whether all groups have been collected.

Check readiness before the opening day

Set an internal check for the stock to be physically available to the booth team, with time for its agreed receiving check. If the required window is at risk, ask the supplier and transport owner which alternatives are feasible before relying on another address or delivery method. Record who can approve a changed route and what confirmed information the carrier or event handler needs.
