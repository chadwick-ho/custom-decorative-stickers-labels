Custom Die-Cut Stickers

Custom die-cut stickers are supplied as individual pieces cut around the finished contour, including the backing. Use this route for artwork, logo and promotional pieces that will be handed out, inserted or sold separately. Specify the complete piece size and quantity per design before choosing how it is packed.

FAQ

Custom Die-Cut Stickers Questions

Can you review the cutline for die-cut stickers?

Yes. Send artwork and size details so the cutline, border width and shape can be checked before quoting.

Can die-cut stickers be packed individually?

Packing can be discussed by project, including loose bulk supply, OPP bags or retail sticker pack requirements.

Which material is best for die-cut stickers?

Material should be confirmed by application. Vinyl, paper, clear film and holographic options can be reviewed based on use and budget.

Project decisions

Count Finished Pieces and Define the Packed Set

A contour cut and a retail set are separate instructions. State how many accepted pieces of each design belong in a bag, backing-card set or bulk group. Identify designs and quantities so a combined print total cannot be mistaken for complete sets.

Show the full cut outline and any narrow protrusions at final size. If a larger backing area is useful for peeling or additional print, compare a kiss-cut arrangement rather than stretching a die-cut description. Material and outdoor or wet-use requirements still need their own construction review.
