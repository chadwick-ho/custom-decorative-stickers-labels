Custom Sticker Sheets

Custom sticker sheets combine multiple peelable designs on a shared backing. They suit stationery ranges, activity products, promotional collections and counted retail sets. Specify the whole sheet, the usable sticker pieces and the number of sheets per pack as three separate quantities.

FAQ

Custom Sticker Sheets Questions

How many stickers can fit on one sheet?

It depends on sheet size, sticker size, spacing and artwork shape. Send your artwork count and target sheet size for review.

Are sticker sheets good for retail packs?

Yes. Sticker sheets can be supplied as single sheets, sets or packed with header cards depending on the project.

Can you help arrange the sheet layout?

Layout review can be discussed after artwork, size and quantity are provided.

Project decisions

Release a Countable Sheet, Then a Complete Set

List each sticker design, its finished dimensions and the required number on the supplied sheet. Mark the outer backing, cutlines, margins and usable peel spacing on one final-size reference. A layout that fits on screen can leave a small sticker difficult to lift after cutting.

State whether the order quantity counts sheets, individual stickers or retail packs. If a pack needs two different sheet versions, identify both and their quantities; one total does not show the intended split. Review the smallest functional piece and longest text before freezing the layout.

Kiss-cut is the cutting method commonly used for these layouts, while a sticker sheet is the delivered grouping. Keep the two decisions connected without treating them as interchangeable product briefs. Send original or authorized artwork, quantities by sheet version and bag/header-card requirements for review.
