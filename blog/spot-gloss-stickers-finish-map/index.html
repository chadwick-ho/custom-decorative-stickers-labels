Spot-Gloss Stickers: Release the Finish Map

Published November 3, 2026.

By ZC Labels · B2B procurement guide · Sources and editorial approach

In this guide
  1. Give color and finish different instructions
  2. Choose how the finish meets the artwork
  3. Approve the finish on the complete construction
  4. Check information and handling

A matte gift label has a glossy fern pattern that appears when the pack turns in the light. The buyer approves the artwork, but the file does not identify which shapes receive the clear finish. For spot-gloss stickers, the procurement decision needs a separate finish map and a physical reference for the intended contrast.

A glossy appearance, a raised texture and an overall protective finish are different requirements. Say which result matters before asking the supplier to quote a process. A clear layer should not be assumed to provide a particular thickness, scratch resistance or tactile feature without confirmation.

Give color and finish different instructions

Keep the approved color artwork and the intended clear-finish areas distinguishable. Ask the producer how to supply the finish channel in its workflow, including any naming and file requirements. A black shape used as a mask should not accidentally become black artwork on the label, and a shiny appearance preview does not define a production channel.

Roland DGA's spot-gloss overview describes clear-ink effects in digital printing. It supports discussing selective finishes as a production option; the actual provider must confirm its available process, material and achievable result. Do not transfer machine-specific settings to another production system.

Choose how the finish meets the artwork

Decide whether gloss follows a printed element, sits inside it or forms an independent decorative pattern. A tight edge-to-edge match creates a different registration question from a broad background texture. Mark the smallest important finish feature and ask the supplier to review it at final size.

Where alignment is critical, request a representative reference and discuss available tolerances with the converter. Avoid inventing a universal gap or line width. If a proposed detail is unreliable, compare a deliberately inset finish, simpler shapes or a larger motif. These alter the design differently and need the buyer's choice.

Design intentionReview question
Gloss follows a printed motifHow is alignment represented and accepted?
Independent clear patternIs it visible in ordinary handling and lighting?
Raised tactile detailWhich process and physical reference establish the feel?
Functional protectionWhat separate evidence supports the required use?

Approve the finish on the complete construction

Review the proposed face material, printing, clear finish and any other protective layer together. An overall laminate can change the contrast the selective treatment was intended to create. Confirm which surface the user actually sees and touches.

Turn a physical reference under relevant ordinary lighting and view it at the intended distance. Touch it if tactile feel is part of the requirement. Keep visual contrast and tactile height as separate observations: a bright highlight does not prove a raised feature. The relief approval guide covers an alternative physical treatment when a formed texture is the actual objective.

Check information and handling

Do not run decoration across important information without reviewing readability. If a barcode or code is involved, assess the final printed and applied piece for its actual task. A digital mask preview cannot demonstrate scan performance.

Retain color and finish file versions, the complete construction reference and accepted sample together. When one layer changes, identify which contrast, handling or use checks need repeating. Use the matte versus glossy guide for overall surface selection; this page concerns a selective treatment within the same design.

For a selective-finish discussion, send final-size artwork, a marked gloss map, the intended visual or tactile result, smallest critical detail, surface and use conditions, quantities and supplied format. Ask for process availability and a representative physical proof before approving the effect.
