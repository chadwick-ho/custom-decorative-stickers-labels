Production planning

Four Decisions That Prevent an Unclear Quote

A useful inquiry connects the artwork to the physical job. The factory can then review trade-offs instead of guessing from a product name.

1. Application

Describe the surface, handling, storage and whether the sticker is applied by hand or in a repeated workflow.

2. Finished structure

Confirm individual pieces, sheets, rolls or retail packs before discussing material and packing.

3. Visual priority

Identify important color, small text, white ink, transparency, border and special-finish expectations.

4. Delivery format

Clarify quantity, sorting, bagging, backing cards, roll direction and destination market.
