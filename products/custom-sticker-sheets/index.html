Custom Sticker Sheets Questions

How many stickers can fit on one sheet?

It depends on sheet size, sticker size, spacing and artwork shape. Send your artwork count and target sheet size for review.

Are sticker sheets good for retail packs?

Yes. Sticker sheets can be supplied as single sheets, sets or packed with header cards depending on the project.

Can you help arrange the sheet layout?

Layout review can be discussed after artwork, size and quantity are provided.
