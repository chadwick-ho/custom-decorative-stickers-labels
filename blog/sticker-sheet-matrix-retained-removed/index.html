Sticker Sheet Matrix: Keep or Remove the Surround?

Published November 15, 2026.

By ZC Labels · B2B procurement guide · Sources and editorial approach

In this guide
  1. Identify the material around each sticker
  2. Compare the complete received sheet
  3. Keep the sample and the commercial scope together

When ordering a multi-design sticker sheet, show whether the material between the cut stickers stays on the sheet or is removed before delivery. If the surrounding print forms part of the retail design, mark that area as intentional. If the buyer wants separate sticker faces sitting on exposed liner, ask for that supplied state to be reviewed and quoted explicitly.

Identify the material around each sticker

The release liner is the backing that carries the adhesive pieces. The material surrounding the cut faces is a different part. Avery Dennison describes that surrounding pressure-sensitive material as the matrix in its explanation of label converting. Removing it leaves the cut labels on the liner. Avery Dennison's labeling value-chain explanation.

For a decorative sheet, retaining the surrounding material can preserve a printed backdrop, collection name or frame. Removing it changes what the shopper sees between the pieces. State which printed elements are meant to remain with the supplied sheet. A light background in a mockup could represent white face material or exposed liner, so label that intent before approving its appearance.

Compare the complete received sheet

Keep the same sticker size, count and backing outline in the comparison. Ask the supplier to identify any proposed change to spacing, construction or finish. If one quote includes removal and another leaves that step to the buyer, add the buyer's handling and rejected-piece allowance to the comparison using its own planning figures.

Buyer removes it laterIdentify who performs the work and review a representative complete sheet through that operation.The task lifts wanted pieces, alters the approved contents or creates unclear accepted quantities.

Review a converted sample where the decision depends on handling. Begin with an unaltered sheet, then try the smallest piece, an edge piece and a piece with a narrow feature. Observe what happens to the neighboring pieces and the supporting liner. Record the particular samples and actions reviewed. Keep an example of any unwanted lifting with its corresponding backing for the supplier's investigation.

Keep the sample and the commercial scope together

If a revised layout or construction is proposed, compare what changes in appearance, handling and finished quantity. Use the kiss-cut separation guide when wanted stickers lift with neighboring material or the liner tears. The supplier needs the observed event and the actual construction to investigate it.

Ask which sample route can represent the chosen supplied state, its charges and its limitations. Keep that answer with the accepted sheet view. ZC sample policy.

For a quotation, send the complete backing dimensions, peelable-piece cutlines and counts, a marked retained-material view, and quantities of finished sheets or packs. Ask which arrangement can be supplied for that artwork and construction. ZC custom sticker sheets.
