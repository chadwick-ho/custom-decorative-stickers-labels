Co-Branded Promotional Stickers: Approve the Shared Layout

Published November 17, 2026.

By ZC Labels · B2B procurement guide · Sources and editorial approach

In this guide
  1. Ask which shared form the partners accept
  2. Check the layout in the space available
  3. Release only the approved layout and its quantity

Sort out the approved logo arrangement before releasing a co-branded promotional sticker. Ask each partner which mark, scale, spacing and background it approves for this use. Put those requirements and the final cut together on one actual-size proof. If the layout cannot fit, ask the partners to approve a different layout, size or placement before production.

Ask which shared form the partners accept

Get the current printable marks from the owners. Ask whether the brief calls for a lead brand and a credit, two peers, or a fixed combined mark supplied by one owner. Record who approves the layout for each party and any venue or campaign edition to which it applies. Use the artwork permissions guide to confirm the separate right to reproduce the files.

The University of Missouri's brand guide gives an example of how one institution handles approved partner logos and what it permits when available space is insufficient. Use each partner's applicable rules for the specific item. A text credit, alternative mark or different arrangement still needs the relevant approval. University of Missouri co-branding guidance.

Check the layout in the space available

Print an actual-size review or use a physical proof when the material and print matter. Place it on the intended gift box or include it in the giveaway pack if the surroundings affect what the viewer sees. A long name and a compact symbol may need different widths to follow both brands' rules. Compare an approved stacked arrangement or a larger contour when the horizontal version crowds a logo. Ask the buyer whether a larger piece would still fit the distribution format.

Release only the approved layout and its quantity

Collect both decisions against the same proof ID. If a partner logo arrives late or a revised mark no longer fits, check which variants can still proceed. Ask the campaign owner and supplier whether holding the affected variant or using an already authorized backup is workable. Confirm any change to counts, packing or timing before issuing the revised order instruction.

Send the approved marks or combined artwork, permitted layouts, proposed finished size and shape, proof approvals still needed, and quantities by variant.
