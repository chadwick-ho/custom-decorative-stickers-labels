Custom Supplement Bottle Labels: Small Text and Control

Published September 26, 2026. A practical guide for supplement brands, contract packers and private-label buyers.

By ZC Labels Production Team · Reviewed for B2B sticker sourcing.

Supplement label projects usually arrive with two competing requests: make the bottle look clean, and fit a large amount of information onto it. The container does not care about either request. It has a fixed circumference, a curve and a cap that may cover more usable height than the artwork assumed.

Our role is not to decide whether a claim is allowed. Our role is to make the approved content printable, readable and controlled from proof through reorder.

Lock the Content Before Polishing the Front

Start with the final approved information set, bottle specification and destination market. Separate fixed text from variable fields such as lot, expiry, flavor or count. If the content is still changing, treat the layout as a working proof, not finished artwork.

Print at actual size early. A panel that feels spacious on a large monitor can become a wall of tiny text on a 60 cc bottle. Decide what must remain on the label and what belongs on an outer carton or insert before reducing type beyond practical reading.

Visual Authority Is Not Regulatory Authority

A supplement bottle can use rich original graphics. Botanical leaves, citrus, berries, mineral shapes, capsules, sun forms and abstract movement can distinguish products without borrowing a medical cross or fake laboratory seal.

The line is straightforward. Graphics can communicate category and energy. They cannot manufacture approval. Do not add doctor recommended, clinically proven, organic or certified marks unless the responsible brand has evidence and approved use.

Build a System That Prevents Picking Errors

A growing range often includes similar white bottles with similar caps. Use a stable brand block and information layout, then give each SKU a distinct original ingredient illustration, accent color and large product name. Color alone is not enough, especially when cartons are packed under warehouse lighting.

Keep the SKU name visible from the front and consider a top or neck cue if bottles are stored in trays. File names, proof names and packing lists should use the same SKU language. Artwork control is not glamorous, but it prevents expensive mistakes.

Test the Complete Coding and Packing Workflow

Apply labels to filled bottles using the planned hand or machine process. Add lot and expiry codes with the real equipment. Rub the code, inspect contrast and pack bottles into the final carton. Check whether dividers or neighboring bottles scuff the print.

If the package includes a tamper band, neck seal or carton, review the whole presentation together. Do not let one component hide another component's required information.

Our Supplement Label Test List

  • Confirm bottle, cap and any outer carton
  • Use one approved content version
  • Print the complete label at actual size
  • Check reading on the curved container
  • Test lot and expiry coding for position and rub
  • Compare every SKU name, graphic and color
  • Run application and carton-abrasion checks
  • Archive the approved proof and production reference

What We Would Put in the Quote Request

Send bottle dimensions and material, label panel, approved artwork, quantities by SKU, application method, variable-code method, finish direction, destination market, document requests and packing configuration. Identify which content has regulatory approval and which fields remain temporary.

The professional look does not come from making the bottle sterile or filling it with badges. It comes from original visual identity, readable information and disciplined version control.
